---
title: "About the Acme Brewer"
description: "The Acme Brewer makes up to eight cups of coffee and keeps it warm for two hours."
---

Fill the tank, add ground coffee to the basket, and press **Brew**. A full pot takes about six minutes.

## What's in the box

- Acme Brewer base with water tank
- Glass carafe and lid
- Reusable mesh filter basket
- Measuring scoop

<Tip>
  Use filtered water to keep the brewer clean for longer.
</Tip>
