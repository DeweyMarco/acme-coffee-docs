---
title: "Cold brew"
description: "Smooth, low-acid coffee for warm days."
---

## You'll need

- 100 g coarsely ground coffee
- 1 liter cold water
- A jar and a fine strainer

## Steps

1. Mix the coffee and water in the jar.
2. Cover and leave it in the fridge for **12 to 18 hours**.
3. Strain and serve over ice.

<Note>Cold brew concentrate keeps in the fridge for up to a week.</Note>

## Scale the recipe

Use this ratio to make any amount of cold brew.

```javascript brew-ratio.js
// 1 g of coffee for every 10 ml of water
const RATIO = 10;

function coldBrew(waterMl) {
  const coffeeGrams = Math.round(waterMl / RATIO);
  return { coffeeGrams, steepHours: 16 };
}

console.log(coldBrew(1000)); // { coffeeGrams: 100, steepHours: 16 }
```
