---
title: "Gift subscriptions"
description: "Buy a prepaid coffee subscription as a gift, or redeem one you received."
---

Anyone can buy a prepaid coffee subscription as a gift. The recipient gets an email with a gift code and redeems it on the Acme Coffee Club site.

## Buy a gift

When you buy a gift, you choose:

- **Length:** 3, 6, or 12 months.
- **Plan:** Solo, Duo, or Office. Gifts use the same [plans](/subscriptions#plans) as regular subscriptions.
- **Send date:** The gift email goes out at **9am Pacific** on the date you pick.

The price is the plan price multiplied by the number of months. Gifts are not discounted.

## Redeem a gift

1. Open the gift email and copy your gift code.
2. On the Acme Coffee Club site, go to **Account > Redeem a gift**.
3. Enter your gift code.

You don't need a card to redeem a gift.

<Warning>
  Gift codes expire 12 months after purchase.
</Warning>

### If you already have a subscription

The gift months are added to your existing subscription. Billing pauses until the gift months are used.

## When a gift ends

Gifts don't renew. Thirty days before your last delivery, you get an email offering a regular subscription.

## Cancel a gift and get a refund

If you bought a gift, you can cancel it for a full refund from **Account > Gifts you sent**, as long as the code hasn't been redeemed. Gifts can't be refunded after the code is redeemed.

## Limitations

- Gifts are subscriptions, not gift cards. You can't buy a gift card with a dollar amount.
- Each gift ships to one address. You can't ship a gift to more than one address.
