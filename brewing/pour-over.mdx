---
title: "Pour-over"
description: "A clean, bright cup, one pour at a time."
---

## You'll need

- 20 g medium-fine ground coffee
- 320 ml water at about 93 °C
- A paper filter and dripper

## Steps

1. Rinse the filter with hot water and discard the water.
2. Add the coffee and pour 40 ml of water to let it bloom for 30 seconds.
3. Pour the rest slowly in circles over 2 minutes.
4. Let it drain, then enjoy.
