---
title: "French press"
description: "A rich, full-bodied cup in four minutes."
---

## You'll need

- 30 g coarsely ground coffee
- 500 ml water just off the boil (about 94 °C)

## Steps

1. Add the coffee to the press.
2. Pour in the water and stir gently.
3. Put the lid on and wait **4 minutes**.
4. Press the plunger down slowly and serve.

<Tip>Pour all the coffee out right away so it doesn't keep brewing.</Tip>
