---
title: "Cold brew"
description: "Smooth, low-acid coffee for warm days."
---

## You'll need

- 100 g coarsely ground coffee
- 1 liter cold water
- A jar and a fine strainer

## Steps

1. Mix the coffee and water in the jar.
2. Cover and leave it in the fridge for **12 to 18 hours**.
3. Strain and serve over ice.

<Note>Cold brew concentrate keeps in the fridge for up to a week.</Note>
